## Featured Work

### [MouthPad^](https://miguelcastroa.github.io/mouthpad)

A tongue-operated interface that helps people with upper-limb disabilities control digital devices with precision.

The work reframed the problem: the barrier was not the person, but the interface. What began as an accessibility idea became a new category of human-computer interaction.

**Recognition:** Cannes Lions Innovation Grand Prix, Cannes Lions Health & Wellness Silver, Titanium Shortlist, CES Innovation Awards Honoree.

### [Vanishing Emails](https://miguelcastroa.github.io/vanishing-emails)

A system for expired promotional emails to disappear when their purpose ended.

A creative idea built from invisible digital waste, using infrastructure as the medium.

**Recognition:** Cannes Lions Innovation Bronze, Titanium Shortlist.

### Bank on Equality

A financial-inclusion idea that turned the wage gap into a debt the bank settled in its own currency.

A product mechanism inside an existing financial system, designed to make social impact real.

**Recognition:** Cannes Lions Bronze, El Ojo Gold, Best Idea Peru, Clio Silver.

### [Behind the Fear](https://miguelcastroa.github.io/behind-the-fear)

A hidden-code campaign designed to help domestic abuse victims reveal distress messages through ordinary digital behavior.

A project about invisible systems, safety and the power of what cannot be seen at first glance.

### En One

A real-time payment feature for micro-merchants in Peru.

Built around a simple belief: financial inclusion works better when it enters the tools and behaviors people already use.

### Nike+

Early connected sports work exploring the overlap between brand, data, community and product behavior.

A project from before those words became category language.

### Hey! He Is Not a Kid

A social-impact campaign built around perception, childhood and the way language changes how people see responsibility.

### IRBIN

A creative technology project exploring how brands can move from communication into platforms, services and useful systems.

## Creative Systems & Frameworks

### Miguel’s Language Model

A creative reasoning framework built around one principle:

**criterion before output.**

It protects the thinking interval before AI makes things feel finished too quickly.

Core nodes include:

* Signal
* Invisible
* Insight
* Tension
* Idea
* Deck
* Case

### Creative R&D

A practice for building with AI without starting from tools.

The sequence:

1. Strategic question
2. Human capacity
3. Intelligence logic
4. Working prototype

### Unseen

A research method for detecting latent pressure before it becomes obvious.

Used to find what the brief is not saying yet.

### Gravity Test

A simple test for whether an idea can hold:

* Gather
* Hold
* Consequence

### Composition Model

A way to turn scattered thinking into a communicable shape.

Built for decks, cases, launches and strategic narratives.
